URSA keeps every self-hosted Uptime Kuma monitor a glance away on your phone.
Connect directly to your server for live state, incident context, management, and
private push without an account, analytics, Google services, or a paid relay.

Features:

* Live monitor state, response-time trends, uptime, heartbeat history, and TLS
  certificate detail.
* Incident, certificate, domain, event, 30-day aggregate, and pinned-live fleet
  views for fast triage.
* Search, quick filters, advanced filters, saved views, favourites, groups, tags,
  compact rows, and responsive tablet layouts.
* Multiple encrypted server connections with password/TOTP or imported sessions,
  optional self-signed TLS, and up to eight custom access headers for Pangolin,
  Cloudflare Access, and similar reverse proxies.
* Create and safely edit common monitor settings, assign notification providers,
  manage groups/tags, run confirmed bulk pause/resume, and manage maintenance.
* UnifiedPush alerts through a distributor you choose, including per-monitor modes,
  severity, quiet hours, delays, repeats, acknowledge/snooze, and managed Kuma setup.
* Slow-response and certificate-expiry alerts generated locally on your device.
* Public status pages with native groups, uptime, recent checks, maintenance, and
  incident history.
* Encrypted portable backups, encrypted offline cache, optional biometric/device
  credential lock, and sensitive screens protected in release builds.
* Native Android extras: widget, Quick Settings tile, shortcuts, scoped deep links,
  notification actions, and an opt-in always-on glance display.
* Light/dark mode with Kuma status colors and optional Material You.
* Optional manual check for stable releases from URSA's official GitHub repository.

A separate Wear OS APK is available from GitHub Releases with a standalone public
dashboard, detail view, tile, complications, and optional same-signed phone pairing
for private monitor actions. The F-Droid phone build remains free of Play Services.

URSA is free and open source with no ads, tracking, premium tier, or subscription.
It is an independent client and is not affiliated with Uptime Kuma.
