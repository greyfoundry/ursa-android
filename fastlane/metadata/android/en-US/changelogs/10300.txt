URSA 1.3.0 adds complete monitoring workflows on phone and Wear OS.

* Added encrypted custom headers for Pangolin, Cloudflare Access, and other proxies.
* Added monitor and maintenance management, bulk actions, groups, tags, and alerts.
* Added incident, certificate, domain, event, aggregate, and pinned-live fleet views.
* Added advanced filters, saved views, secure backups, and improved connection help.
* Added a Wear OS dashboard, monitor details, tile, complications, and optional pairing.
* Refreshed the interface and verified compatibility through Uptime Kuma 2.5.3.
